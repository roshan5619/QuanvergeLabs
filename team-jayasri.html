CEO & Chief Scientist

Dr. Jayasri Dontabhaktuni

Professor of Physics and Marie-Curie Fellow. Her research spans all-dielectric metamaterials, liquid crystals and Monte Carlo simulation, and more recently the quantum machine learning methods that underpin the Quanverge product suite.
